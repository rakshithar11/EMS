About Bobba Group

Who we are, and what we do.

Who We Are

With a presence of over 30 years and a team of more than 500 people, Bobba Group has built its business on the core values of efficiency, honesty, trust, and commitment. The group has interests in aviation, airport cargo terminal handling, ground ramp and passenger handling, warehousing, and logistics.

Bobba Group brings quality solutions for freight brokerage, route planning, transportation management, supply chain management, GPS fleet tracking, and global trade compliance — powered by the latest technology to keep operations time-bound and hassle-free without compromising on quality.

Beyond its commercial ventures, Bobba Group is also involved in philanthropy, supporting old age homes and providing educational assistance to the economically weak and the physically challenged.

History

Bobba Group started out as the general sales & service agent for the leading European carrier Lufthansa Cargo in South and East India, a role it held for almost 23 years. Best-quality products and service have been its hallmark since inception.

Vision

To build a highly motivated service organization spanning every service vertical it operates in — cargo terminals in the air freight industry, logistics, and warehousing — with an aim to deliver best-in-class projects.
